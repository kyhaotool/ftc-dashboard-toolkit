Flywheel Formulas

1. SHAFT RPM
Shaft RPM = (Motor RPM / Gear Ratio) × Efficiency
2. SURFACE SPEED
Surface Speed (m/s) = Shaft RPM × π × Diameter / 60
3. MOMENT OF INERTIA
I = 0.5 × m × r²
m = density × π × r² × width
4. SPIN-UP TIME
t = (ω_target × 0.9) / α
α = Torque / I
5. FEEDFORWARD
kV = 1 / (kV_motor × efficiency × gear_ratio)

PID Formulas

ZIEGLER-NICHOLS
Kp = 0.6 × Ku
Ki = 2 × Kp / Tu
Kd = Kp × Tu / 8
Ku = Ultimate Gain, Tu = Ultimate Period
PIDF OUTPUT
Output = P×error + I×∫error + D×derror/dt + FF
FF = kS×sign(v) + kV×v + kA×a
